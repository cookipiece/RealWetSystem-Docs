# Purchase activation

<ProductName /> needs purchase activation once per PC. Open the activation window from **CookiTools > RealWetSystem > Purchase Activation** in the Unity menu bar, enter your BOOTH order number (or your Gumroad license key if you bought it on Gumroad), then press **Activate**. When **Activation complete.** appears, you're done.

{/* SHOT U02: Purchase Activation window */}
[![The Purchase Activation window, not activated yet](/img/guide/U02-en.png)](/img/guide/U02-en.png)

- You can choose English / 한국어 / 日本語 at the top right of the activation window. The language you choose also applies to the <ProductName /> windows.
- You need the internet when you activate, and at least once every 3 weeks for automatic renewal. Building and uploading avatars doesn't need it.
- Purchase activation works only in the Unity Editor on Windows.
- A code you received from Cooki (starting with `CKR-`) goes in the same field. Please don't share the code with others.

## What needs activation {/* #what-is-gated */}

- **New install:** Activation is needed only when you press **Install** in the setup window on an avatar that doesn't have <ProductName /> yet.
- If you press **Install** without activating, a notice with an **Open activation window** button appears, and the avatar is not changed.

## What it doesn't block {/* #not-blocked */}

- Adding outfits, changing settings, **Update**, **Uninstall — Remove**
- The Unity preview and Play
- Building and uploading avatars. Purchase activation never stops a build.
- Even an activation that couldn't be renewed because the PC was offline for a long time doesn't leave the effects out of uploads. Until you go online and renew, only new installs are blocked.

## Uploading from a PC that is not activated {/* #uploads */}

If you upload from a PC that is not activated, the <ProductName /> effects are left out of the uploaded avatar.

- The build doesn't fail, and no window appears. A one-line warning starting with `RealWetSystem:` appears in the Console.
- The avatar in the scene, the preview and Play don't change. Activate and upload again, and the effects are included.
- When the effects would be left out of an upload, a notice and an **Open activation window** button appear right below the Avatar field of the setup window.
- <ProductName /> also draws clothes getting wet in RealRainSystem's rain, so that is left out too. RealRain's weather (clouds, rain, rain sound) depends only on RealRain's activation.

How to check is described in [Uploaded without purchase activation](./troubleshooting.mdx#not-activated).

## Number of PCs and releasing a PC {/* #pcs */}

- With one order, you can activate up to 3 PCs with a personal license, and up to 10 PCs with a Game & App Integration & Distribution License.
- There is also a 30-day limit on how many new PCs one order can register. For the exact numbers, please see the shared guide.
- You can free up a PC you no longer use with **Release this PC** in the activation window. Pressing it before you replace a PC or reinstall Windows saves one of your PC slots.
- Purchase activation is done separately for each tool. Please enter RealSweatSystem or RealRainSystem order numbers in that tool's activation window.

## If activation doesn't work

Please press **Copy report** in the activation window and contact Cooki. Your order number is not included in the report.
